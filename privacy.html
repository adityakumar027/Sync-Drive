Privacy Policy for Sync Drive

Effective Date: October 18, 2026
Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Sync Drive ("we", "our", or "the app") is an open-source Android application designed to mirror local device folders to your personal Google Drive account.

1. Information We Access and How It Is Used

  • Google Sign-In & Authentication: The app uses Google Sign-In to authenticate your account and obtain an OAuth2 access token. We access your email address solely to display your active account in the application.
  • Google Drive Data: The app accesses your Google Drive solely to upload files and mirror folder structures from your local device to the destination Drive folders you specify.
  • Local Device Storage: The app requests storage permissions exclusively to scan, read, and upload the folders and files that you select for synchronization.

2. Data Storage and Transfer

No Third-Party Servers: All file transfers and API requests occur directly between your Android device and Google's official servers (googleapis.com). We do not operate intermediary servers, databases, or analytics services. Your files, tokens, and metadata are never transmitted to us or any third party.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Sync Drive's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, contact us via GitHub Issues at: Sync Drive Issues.
